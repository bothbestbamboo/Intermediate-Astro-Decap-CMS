BothBest's Product:

Bamboo Poles

Sep 12, 2026, 11:30 AM

Bamboo, an ancient plant on the earth, Latin name is Phyllostachys Pubescens, in China, it is called Mao Zhu or Mao Bamboo. Bamboo has 1200 species, including moso bamboo, black bamboo, red bamboo, bleached bamboo. China has about 500 species.

Bamboo in China grows up in south of Yangtze River because it has plenty of sunshine and rain, average temperature is 15-20 Centigrade and yearly rain is 1200-2000mm. Bamboo grows up fast because of enough water. China has 4 million acres of bamboo which is 50% of the world, in China, 47% of bamboo is Mao bamboo or Moso bamboo. Zhejiang, Fujian, JIangxi, Hunan and Anhui are most of bamboo area.

Bamboo grows fast. In the beginning months, it can grow 40cm per day and mature bamboo is 35-40m tall. Bamboo can be harvested after 4-6 years. Density of bamboo is 500-700 kg / m3. Bamboo is called Plant Steel which means it has hard and flexible for various constructions. Bamboo is used for the roof in World Expo in Shanghai to save energy cost and unique design.

Bamboo is cut down from the mountain, selected by length and diameter. Leafs will be removed by hands. Boil bamboo stick in 4 hours to remove the sugar inside of bamboo. Bamboo need get straight by special tool. After boiling, bamboo will get dry under sunshine, this takes 30-45 days to get complete dry. Too wet bamboo will be moldy.

Bamboo poles are sustainably harvested from 3-5 year old bamboo in order to achieve maximum wall thickness and density. Every bamboo pole undergoes a natural curing process to ensure strength, longevity, and beauty. Bamboo poles possess incredible structural properties allowing for innovative construction applications. Since bamboo is hollow and designed by nature to bend, it can be incorporated into unique design elements that cannot be achieved with most other structural materials.

Bamboo stick will be packed by 100 pieces per bundle with nylon bag. Bamboo pole can be for garden as fencing, for support of grape, olive tree, for the support of construction.

Standard length is 2.2m, 2.9m, 5.8m.

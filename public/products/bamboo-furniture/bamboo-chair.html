Bamboo Chair Made of Bamboo Plywood

Sep 23, 2026, 8:30 AM

Bamboo chair in most design is made by big bamboo poles, this is for outdoor seating, like restaurants, bar. This kind of chair is easy to be broken after a few months. We use strand woven bamboo to make very durable chair which is more suitable for outdoor and indoor, like kitchen, studying room. Bamboo chair can match with your home decoration perfectly with its beautiful color and grain.

Bamboo chair can be natural bamboo color or stained with various unique colors, like red, black. Bamboo chair can be made with custom designs, like western styles or traditional Chinese styles. Bamboo chair can have cushion on the seat and back support.

Features:

- 1) Made with premium strand woven bamboo, high density, durable

- 2) Custom designs to match with your house style or flooring matching

- 3) Prefinished and well-treated with environmentally finishing

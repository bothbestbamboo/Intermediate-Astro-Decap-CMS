Bamboo panel is another great product because it is a very good opportunity to replace regular wood plywood for the furniture production, like table, chairs, kitchen cabinet. Bamboo furniture is introduced in the world even earlier than bamboo flooring, but that furniture is made of bamboo pole. Bamboo furniture produced from strand woven is even better quality than Mahogany furniture because of its super high density.

BothBest's Product:

Bamboo Construction Plywood Formwork Board

Sep 23, 2026, 8:30 AM

Bamboo construction plywood board is produced from bamboo strips with high pressure - 3 - 4 MPa and high temperature - 130 Centigrade. Bamboo plywood is pressed with environmentally glue with E2 standard VOC. Bothbest produced a series of bamboo plywood board with the features of high strength, light weight, stiffness, smooth, water-resistant, abrasion resistance, fewer joints, low thermal conductivity, no rust, no distortion, easy to remove from the mold, low shrinkage and low water absorption, can be the level of mold, mold wall, column model, beam model, versatility, can greatly reduce the labor intensity and reduce the amount of template inputs, improve efficiency, shorten the construction period, to achieve better economic efficiency.

Bamboo construction plywood - film faced

Bamboo construction plywood board features
  • Bamboo plywood construction board is high strength and good toughness. The static strength is 8 to 10 times than wood board, 4 to 5 times than wood plywood, can reduce the number of board support.
  • Bamboo plywood construction board is wide, less patchwork. Standard sheet size is 2440 x 1220 mm, equivalent to 6.6 pieces of 1500 x 300 mm of steel formwork, can save removal time.
  • Bamboo plywood construction board is smooth surface, easy to remove from the mold. Bamboo plywood is film faced, the adsorption of concrete is only 1/8 of steel formwork.
  • Bamboo construction plywood board is water resistance. Board doesn't split after boiled three hours and board doesn't swear in the concrete curing process.
  • Bamboo plywood board is anticorrosion and anti-moth-eaten.
  • Thermal conductivity of bamboo plywood board is 0.14-0.16 meters, far less than the thermal conductivity of steel formwork, is conducive to winter construction insulation.
  • Bamboo plywood board is recycling, both surface and bottom can be used, with correct using for the board, recycled time is up to 10-30 times.
Bamboo construction plywood board usage:

High strength bamboo plywood board can be level board in the high building, shear walls and vertical templates, also is very appropriate for the highway, overpass, viaduct, dam, tunnel concrete formwork. Bamboo plywood can be the train, car trunk flooring, container flooring, the trailer wall and the package panel.

Bamboo construction plywood - film faced

How to saw bamboo construction plywood board:

Saw has to be the alloy saw blade with diameter of 25 to 400 mm, about 120 teeth, speed 4000 r / min.

Bamboo construction plywood board dimension:
  • Length x Width: 2440 x 1220 mm
  • Thickness: 6 mm, 12 mm, 15 mm, 18 mm

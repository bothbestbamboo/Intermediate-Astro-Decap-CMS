## 🏭 Manufacturing Process

Our bamboo drink stirrers are sustainably produced using prime, mature Moso bamboo culms sourced directly from managed bamboo plantations[cite: 32]:

1. **Harvesting & Slicing:** Mature bamboo stalks are selected, split into fine strips, and shaved to uniform thickness[cite: 32].
2. **High-Temperature Sterilization:** Bamboo strips undergo high-temperature steaming and drying to eliminate natural sugars, prevent mold, and ensure food-contact hygiene[cite: 32].
3. **Precision Laser & Die Cutting:** Advanced CNC and laser-cutting equipment shape the custom top icons (pineapples, guitars, custom brand logos) and cut precise stir stick lengths[cite: 32].
4. **Multi-Stage Polishing & Deburring:** Sticks undergo extensive mechanical tumbling to remove burrs, ensuring a silky-smooth, splinter-free finish[cite: 32].
5. **Food-Grade Quality Control & Packaging:** Inspected for structural consistency, packed in hygienic bulk or eco-friendly individual sleeves, ready for global distribution[cite: 32].

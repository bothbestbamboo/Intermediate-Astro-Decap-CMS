## 📝 Product Overview

**Flexible Bamboo Wall Covering & Mat Rolls** (also commonly referred to as bamboo slats matting or bamboo wallpaper) combine natural aesthetic warmth with exceptional design flexibility. Crafted from precision-milled bamboo slats backed by a durable fabric lining, these rolls can easily wrap around flat walls, curved surfaces, columns, and custom furniture.

Designed for convenient installation and long-lasting performance, our bamboo rolls offer an eco-conscious, textured architectural finish suitable for residential, commercial, and hospitality interior projects.

## 💎 Key Advantages

- **High Flexibility & Versatility:** Smooth fabric backing allows the bamboo roll to curve seamlessly around pillars, rounded walls, and contoured interior surfaces without cracking.
- **Rich Aesthetic Options:** Available in natural raw bamboo, rich carbonized tones, and unique decorative tiger/burnt patterns to match diverse architectural interior styles.
- **Easy Installation & Customization:** Can be effortlessly trimmed to desired sizes and fixed to walls or backing boards using standard adhesives or micro-fasteners.
- **Sustainable & Durable:** Made from fast-growing, highly renewable bamboo with protective surface treatments for enhanced resistance against wear and fading.

---

## 🏭 Manufacturing Process

Our flexible bamboo wall coverings and mats are produced through a rigorous quality-controlled process:

1. **Bamboo Slat Milling:** Premium mature bamboo stalks are harvested, split, and planed into exact slat widths of 8mm, 11mm, or 16mm.
2. **Thermal Treatment & Coloring:** Slats undergo high-temperature carbonization, natural drying, or flame treatment to produce uniform natural, carbonized, or tiger-pattern finishes.
3. **Lamination & Fabric Assembly:** Bamboo slats are neatly aligned and securely bonded onto a sturdy, flexible textile or gauze backing under controlled tension.
4. **Precision Trimming & Rolling:** Finished sheets are cut to standard widths of 1.8m or 2.1m and rolled into continuous 15m lengths.
5. **Quality Inspection & Packaging:** Each roll is inspected for surface consistency, alignment, and backing adhesion before protective wrapping.

## 🗺️ Primary Applications

Thanks to its natural elegance and flexible structure, bamboo wall covering transforms various indoor spaces:

- **Accent Walls & Vertical Panels:** Adds rich tactile texture and organic warmth to living room feature walls, reception desks, and hotel lobbies.
- **Column & Curved Surface Cladding:** Smoothly wraps round architectural columns and curved corridors where rigid panels cannot be applied.
- **Ceiling Cladding & Accents:** Provides a lightweight, cozy natural finish for decorative ceiling soffits, resort pavilions, and teahouse ceilings.
- **Wainscoting & Cabinet Facing:** Works as durable wall protection wainscoting or decorative door inserts for custom cabinetry and furniture design.

An Overview of Strand Woven Bamboo Flooring and its Benefits

Sep 12, 2026, 11:30 AM

There are numerous kinds of bamboo floors available in the market now. So, with so many options available, people can end up being confused regarding which is the best option for them. The Strand Woven Bamboo Flooring is often termed as the best in the market due to their superb characteristics. They stand out in the market due to their unique construction as well as ways of pre-processing that they go through before they end up in a person's house.

Why are they considered to be one of the best in the market?

Generally, a bamboo plank is constructed in a laminate-like way by using thin and flat cut layers of bamboo. They are then pressed together for forming the ultimate product. However, unlike the normal construction method, a Strand Woven Bamboo Flooring is created from shredded fibers from canes and is later woven together for forming a denser product. The product is then put under pressure and heat and along with the proper usage of adhesives, they are turned into extremely durable material for construction.

They are just beautiful to view as well as provide an original look to the floor made of it. With some more processing, they either preserve their natural look or are carbonized for forming up an altered finish. They can also be stained for providing a huge number of themes as well as coloring available.

People who are looking forward to getting a guarantee for a supreme quality floor as well as high durability, the Strand Woven Bamboo Flooring is the best solution for them. There are no doubts about its strength and style and is also a boon for the people as well as the environment who respect them. So, this type of bamboo flooring is the best idea for people.

What are the benefits of using this type of bamboo flooring?

Material – Bamboo is one of the most preferred materials when it comes to flooring. They do not take much time to grow as well as to mature, unlike hardwood trees. The hardwood varieties such as oak trees take a lot of time to reach the maturity level. Also, every time hardwood trees are used for the purpose of construction, the risk of forest depletion is increased. So, ultimately the environment is harmed. On the other hand, a bamboo grows within a period of 4-5 years and is available readily. They are found abundantly in maximum Southeast Asian countries.

Unique design – The strand woven bamboo flooring has unique designs. So, while choosing bamboos for the purpose of flooring, most people choose this particular type over others. They have various types of design patterns. In their manufacturing process, a high-quality adhesive is used for woven the strands, and this creates a sturdy plank along with a high density that makes them able to withstand high pressure. Therefore, they are ideal for places that enjoy high traffic like lobbies as well as areas in the home such as the living room.

Low cost – bamboo flooring has a lower cost when compared to other kinds of flooring materials. Whenever a person is looking forward to buying flooring material, they should always consider the factor of cost as the primary factor. This factor determines whether a material is suitable or not according to monetary terms.

Bamboo flooring is exotic and has a unique appeal to people. However, despite these facts, they have a comparatively lower cost when compared to other types of flooring such as hardwood flooring. They come at such a price that can be afforded by most of the people. So, this is one of the biggest benefits of using this type of bamboo for flooring.

Easy to maintain – every flooring material must have the ability to be easily cleaned and maintained. So, it is an essential tip for people to choose those flooring materials that they can clean easily and hence as a result does not face any difficulty in maintaining. So, people who are looking for the best quality flooring material can go for the strand woven bamboo flooring. They are easy to clean and hence can be maintained easily. They just need to be cleaned with mild soap as well as a mop. There is no need of any special treatment. So, they serve as a great product.

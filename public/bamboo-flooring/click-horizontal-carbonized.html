Horizontal in click Bamboo flooring is all 3 layers flat pressed, wear layer is 4mm thickness of horizontal bamboo, core is Chinese pine and bottom layer is Chinese poplar. Tongue and groove is Unilin click lock system.

Carbonized color has a darker color tone when compared to natural bamboo color. It is also called caramel color as it is a little dark and honey.

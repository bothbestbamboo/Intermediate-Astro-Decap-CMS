## 🏭 Manufacturing Process

Our bamboo tumbler sleeves undergo a specialized technical process to balance natural beauty with industrial longevity[cite: 31]:

1. **Veneer Selection & Slicing:** Premium natural bamboo is sliced into ultra-thin, flexible wood veneers with consistent grain pattern and color[cite: 31].
2. **Adhesive Application:** Food-grade German Jowat hot-melt adhesive is evenly applied across bamboo layers under strict temperature control[cite: 31].
3. **Multi-Layer Roll-Wrapping:** Veneers are tightly wrapped in continuous cross-laminated layers onto precision cylindrical molds[cite: 31].
4. **Thermal Bonding & Pressing:** High pressure and heat cure the adhesive, creating a seamless, ultra-dense cylindrical shell[cite: 31].
5. **Precision Trimming & CNC Polishing:** Shells are cut to exact φ82x115mm measurements, smooth-sanded, and treated with protective food-safe surface sealing[cite: 31].

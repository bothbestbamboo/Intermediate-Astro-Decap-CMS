Dark Strand Woven bamboo decking or heat treated bamboo decking is a new type and patented product of regular strand woven bamboo flooring. We are doing super dark carbonizing process than regular carbonizing for bamboo strips. Dark carbonized bamboo strips are deep dark, high stability and low moisture. By using dark carbonized bamboo strips for making bamboo flooring, it is more stable and uniform color than regular bamboo flooring.

Dark strand woven is a much stronger bamboo flooring compared with strand woven, solid bamboo and engineered bamboo, even harder than most of hardwood, like oak, teak, jatoba etc.

Dark strand woven block is a nice solution for terrace flooring, decking, outdoor flooring. Ideal for high traffic residential and commercial settings, super strand woven bamboo flooring can be used where other woods like oak, fail to perform. Super strand bamboo is water proof.

Hardness: 4000 per Janka scale

The key difference between regular bamboo with dark strand bamboo is not on the glue, but the special treatment on bamboo strips. Regular bamboo is 4 hours carbonizing, and dark strand bamboo is 12 hours carbonizing, so the carbonization is very different and this makes bamboo look very dark, almost black color. With long time carbonizing, it will get bamboo strips very dry and moisture is super low, almost zero, so after pressing with glue, the decking is very stable and no bending, moldy and splitting.

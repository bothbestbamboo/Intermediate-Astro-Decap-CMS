​Bamboo Decking Reviews

Raw Material: Bamboo, often referred to as the 'green steel' of the 21st Century, has gained a significant amount of popularity and for good reasons too. Not only is it renowned for its inherent strength and durability, but it also presents an environmentally friendly solution to traditional timber. The natural composition of bamboo provides an extraordinary degree of hardness.

Strand Woven Bamboo: Bamboo decking is manufactured on basis of strand woven bamboo which is made through a unique process that involves shredding bamboo fibers, which are then compressed under high temperature and pressure. This process not only recycles what would otherwise be waste material but also results in a product that is twice as durable as regular bamboo and rivals the hardness of the most robust hardwoods.

Exceptional Durability: The intense compression process increases its density, making it incredibly hard and resistant to wear and tear. This density not only contributes to its longevity but also ensures it can withstand fluctuating temperatures, heavy foot traffic, and even the demands of commercial installations.

Aesthetic Versatility: It offers a unique and sophisticated appearance that can seamlessly blend with various architectural styles, for a modern chic terrace or a cozy, rustic outdoor space, from a luxurious resort-style deck to a minimalist contemporary design.

Bamboo decking can withstand the wear and tear brought by heavy foot traffic, harsh weather conditions, and time itself. Strand woven bamboo decking emerges as a standout choice for outdoor living spaces. Revered for its exceptional quality, this material combines the natural beauty of bamboo with a manufacturing process that enhances its strength, making it a viable competitor to traditional wood decking options.

​Bamboo Decking Suppliers Reviews

The number of bamboo decking providers offering a variety of goods to satisfy different needs in terms of quality, price, and sustainability is increasing along with the popularity of bamboo decking. It's critical to comprehend the qualities of a quality bamboo decking supplier before reading the reviews.

  • 1. Bamboo's quality

    Bamboo decking comes in several varieties. Because Moso bamboo is so strong and long-lasting, it is usually used to make the best bamboo decking. Bamboo that has been adequately treated to withstand moisture, pests, and UV rays should be available from suppliers. Seek out goods that are engineered for increased strength or that are strand-woven.

  • 2. Certifications for Sustainability

    Although bamboo's sustainability is a major factor in bamboo selection, not all bamboo is harvested ethically. Reputable vendors should be able to offer sustainability certifications from groups like ISO 14001 or the Forest Stewardship Council (FSC), which guarantee that the bamboo comes from forests that are responsibly maintained and harvested.

  • 3. Sturdiness and Care

    Bamboo decking must be able to endure exposure to the elements, such as dampness, sunlight, and temperature changes. The top vendors provide bamboo decking that has undergone carbonization, pressure, or heat treatment to make it extremely resistant to insect damage, weathering, and decay.

  • 4. Warranty and Customer Service

    A reliable supplier should provide a strong warranty and first-rate customer service to support their goods. Because bamboo decking is an investment, it's critical to pick a supplier who provides comprehensive installation instructions, upkeep advice, and assistance in the event of problems.

There are numerous top-notch suppliers of bamboo decking available, each providing premium, long-lasting, and environmentally responsible solutions. There is a supplier that can satisfy your needs, regardless of whether sustainability certificates, warranty protection, or the visual appeal of your decking are your top priorities.

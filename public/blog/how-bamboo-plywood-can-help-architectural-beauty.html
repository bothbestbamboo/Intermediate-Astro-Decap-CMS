How Bamboo Plywood Can Help Architectural Beauty?

The architecture of any house is crucial as there are too many factors to look out for. In case, you are buying an apartment and you just have to renovate the house, you can make it just the way you want it to be. Plywood made out of Bamboo is getting a lot of attention these days because of its nature-friendly aspect. We seek to use natural resources and creating a better product for a better world.

Made from 100% rapidly renewable bamboo, the bamboo plywood can be cut and sanded using conventional woodworking equipment. It can also be glued or mechanically fastened using the same materials and fasteners commonly used with wood. Bamboo is as dense as the hardest hardwoods in the world which makes it water resistant, moisture resistant and sustainable.

Plywood made of bamboo is produced quite similarly like bamboo veneer. There is no shortage of bamboos in the market, thus, easily available. Bamboo can grow to its full length in 60 days in comparison to the decades which wood takes to grow to its full length. After growing to the fullest lengths, bamboo forms a very hard, lightweight, and durable wood when treated.

Available in both an amber and natural light tone, we can avail you a lot of different shades sanded. Along with 180-grit and a low-resin, open-grained material that takes stains and finishes exceptionally well. Behind this gorgeous material, bamboo has a certain charm for sleek aesthetic and an ingenious combination of furniture creations that ensures a purposeful place in the home.

The potential bamboo has is unmatched with other materials. These are even stronger and more durable than wood. Clean, modern lines and eco designs are designed for functionality.

Bamboo Decking
  • Environmental protection: Bamboo is a natural material that grows rapidly, so the production process of bamboo decking and the environment will not have much impact during use. In addition, after the service life is over, the bamboo decking can also be recovered and reused, thereby reducing the impact of waste on the environment.
  • Strong durability: After special thermo treatment on carbonized bamboo strips, bamboo decking has good weather resistance and waterproof performance, so the service life of outdoor bamboo decking is long. After pre-oiled in the factory, bamboo decking can be suitable for various outdoor environments.

How Strong is Bamboo Plywood?

Bamboo is the most beautiful material to use because of its natural rich color variations. The surface finish is smooth and the material is very hard, it is so similar to hardwoods such as Walnut or Oak. Since Bamboo grows so fast that it can produce 20 times more than trees, it has a very short cropping cycle of between 4 and 6 years. So, before you can cut the trees after 30-60 years, bamboos can built an entire empire. Once the bamboo is cut it starts growing again straight away without needing to have the roots pulled up and the ground replanting which also reduce erosion and soil quality because it stays undisturbed for the lifetime of the bamboo. Therefore, it is much environment friendly to purchase bamboo plywood than the wooden ones.

Bamboo is strong and returns back into the earth at the end of it’s long life. These materials are as dense as the hardest hardwoods in the world, which also makes it more water resistant, longer lasting and more sustainable. Bamboo is more moisture resistant, harder and more stable than oak. It produces 35% more oxygen than deciduous trees which makes it more efficient at cleaning the polluted air around us.

Bamboo plywood is well known for being called as strong as concrete in compression and easy to work with as it is lighter than wood, concrete or steel. Bamboo Plywood can be cut with regular tools but if you are cutting with a table saw, you can tape the edges to prevent flaking or tearing. If you are fastening the bamboo, be sure to pre-drill any holes as it may split easily if you don’t pre-drill. Regular wood adhesives can be used as well and use sand before applying glue for better adhesion.

Bamboo is one of the finest and classiest materials to make the house flooring with it. This fibrous and an organic material is made out of a grass, not wood. Bamboo plywood is very durable as the hardest hardwoods in the world. The utilization of bamboo plywood can influence the outline of decks while decreasing its effect on nature as it generates more oxygen, making your living and working climate more secure and healthier.

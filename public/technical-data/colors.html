Bamboo Flooring Colors

Sep 12, 2026, 11:30 AM

Bamboo flooring offers a range of colors and styles to suit various preferences and interior design schemes. Bamboo flooring has 2 basic colors, Natural and Carbonized.

Natural color: The natural color of bamboo is light yellow. It is the original color of bamboo strips. When bamboo is heated, different colors are produced. The more you heat the bamboo, the darker the color gets, and the bamboo becomes softer and less durable. So, choose a lighter shade for the floor, if you want a natural and more durable flooring. Natural bamboo flooring retains the light, blond color of natural bamboo. It showcases the characteristic bamboo grain patterns and nodes, providing a clean and minimalist look. Natural bamboo is often preferred for its simplicity and versatility, blending well with various decor styles.

Carbonized color: The carbonized color of Bamboo flooring is steamed color of bamboo strips. Carbonized bamboo flooring comes in shades of coffee, caramel and honey. These different colors are obtained by boiling bamboo at different temperatures. This makes it around 20% softer. So it is not so durable compared to natural bamboo floor. But, if you are looking for a specific color to match your home decor, carbonized bamboo flooring is a good choice.

Stained: Bamboo flooring can be stained in various colors to mimic the look of traditional hardwoods such as oak, walnut, or mahogany. Stained bamboo flooring offers a wider range of color options, allowing homeowners to achieve specific looks, but stained bamboo will have a special requirement on quantity, small quantity won't be accepted for production.

Tiger Strand: Tiger strand bamboo flooring is created by combining natural and carbonized bamboo strips during the manufacturing process. This creates a distinctive striped pattern that resembles tiger stripes, hence the name. Tiger strand bamboo flooring adds a bold and unique look to interior spaces.

Distressed: Distressed bamboo flooring is treated to give it a weathered, aged appearance. This style is popular for creating a rustic or antique look in interior spaces. Distressed bamboo flooring is available in various colors and finishes to complement different design themes.

Most Important is Natural Variations: Bamboo is a natural material, and as such, there can be variations in color and grain patterns even within the same batch of flooring. This natural variation adds to the beauty and uniqueness of bamboo flooring, making each floor a one-of-a-kind piece.

Dasso Bamboo Flooring - Innovator and Expert

Sep 12, 2026, 11:30 AM

Hangzhou Dasso Flooring Co., Ltd was created by Mr. Larry Lin in 1993 and now we have over 1000 employees with 80,000 sq m of factory area, annual sales revenue is 24 million us dollar per year. Our customers are from Europe, American, Australia. Chain stores, like Homedepot, OBI. Importer and distributor, like Manington, MOSO, Shaw. We have offices worldwide, in Europe, we have the warehouse in Hungary; in USA, we have warehouse in Atlantic. The 2 big warehouses will ensure us make the delivery directly to the client quickly and fast, so the customers need not wait for 1-2 months from China to Europe or USA. In China, we have 1100 acres of bamboo forest as the raw materials for producing bamboo flooring and bamboo panel. Some of bamboo forests are FSC certified in 2009. We have 6 braches for producing different bamboo products.

We also co-operated with many universities for researching and developing bamboo, like Chinese Academy of Forestry, Nanjing Forestry University, Zhejiang University, The University of Cambridge, University of Helsinki etc.

Dasso bamboo had been widely designed and applied into several famous projects.
  • 2003, bamboo ceiling was applied into Madrid Barajas Airport, 230,000 m2 in roof construction. Bamboo veneer ceiling reached European highest fire safety standard.

    Madrid Barajas Airport - Terminal 4

  • 2004, outdoor bamboo decking was applied into World Expo 2010 Shanghai.

    World Expo 2010 - Shanghai

  • 2006, bamboo veneer was applied in BMW X5 interior dashboard.

    BMW X5 - Dashboard

  • 2007, high-strength laminated bamboo wind turbines.

    Bamboo Wind Turbines

  • 2008, bamboo flooring and panel were applied in Beijing MOMA project with 70,000 m2.

Dasso is one of biggest bamboo products factory, we are not manufacturing the largest quantity, but we did make the largest research and development. Dasso's aim is: To provide high quality and environmental and healthy bamboo decorative materials for the world.

Bothbest is second position than Dasso in China because Dasso is famous in developing and manufacturing and Bothbest is famous for marketing and production. Dasso manufactures one type of bamboo in 30 containers per month and Bothbest can manufacture various bamboo products in 30 containers per month.

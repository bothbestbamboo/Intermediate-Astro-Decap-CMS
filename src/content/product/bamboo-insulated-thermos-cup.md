## 🏭 Manufacturing Process

Our bamboo insulated thermos cups are produced through a controlled manufacturing process that combines bamboo processing, stainless steel forming, accessory assembly, and product inspection.

1. **Bamboo Material Selection:** Suitable bamboo sections are selected according to fiber appearance, wall quality, surface condition, and overall suitability for a cylindrical outer shell. Material with severe cracking, deformation, or visible defects is removed during inspection.

2. **Bamboo Cutting and Forming:** The bamboo is cut and processed into the required outer sleeve components. The inner opening, outer diameter, lid fit, and bottom structure are prepared according to the product design.

3. **Drying and Conditioning:** Bamboo components are dried and conditioned to help reduce excess moisture and improve dimensional stability during assembly, storage, and normal indoor use.

4. **Surface Sanding:** The outer bamboo surface is sanded through controlled stages to create a smooth hand feel. Edges and contact areas are refined to reduce rough fibers and improve the overall finish.

5. **Protective Surface Treatment:** A suitable protective finish is applied to help protect the bamboo exterior from normal handling, light moisture, and everyday surface contamination. The final finish may vary according to customer requirements.

6. **Stainless Steel Inner Liner Production:** The 304 stainless steel inner liner is formed, polished, and prepared for integration with the bamboo exterior. The drinking surface is checked for smoothness and visible production defects.

7. **Lid and Infuser Assembly:** The lid, sealing ring, metal components, and removable mesh tea infuser are assembled according to the selected style. Carrying handles, loops, or straps are added when included in the product configuration.

8. **Fit and Seal Inspection:** The lid is checked for proper engagement with the cup body. The silicone ring is inspected for correct placement, and sample units may be tested for leakage under appropriate conditions.

9. **Appearance and Function Inspection:** Finished cups are reviewed for bamboo surface quality, liner condition, lid alignment, infuser fit, base stability, and overall appearance before packing.

10. **Packing and Shipment:** Approved products are packed with protective materials to reduce surface rubbing, impact, and movement during storage and transportation.

## 🧼 Care and Use Recommendations

Wash the stainless steel inner liner, lid, and infuser before first use. After drinking tea or coffee, rinse the interior and remove the infuser so that residue does not remain trapped in the mesh.

Use a soft sponge or bottle brush with mild dish soap for routine cleaning. The lid and infuser should be separated when possible to allow the sealing ring and mesh to dry thoroughly. Do not leave tea, coffee, or other beverages inside the cup for extended periods.

The bamboo exterior should not be soaked in water or placed in a dishwasher unless the specific product model has been designed and approved for that cleaning method. Wipe the exterior with a damp cloth and dry it promptly. Avoid abrasive pads, strong solvents, prolonged direct sunlight, and extreme heat.

The cup is intended for normal beverage use. Customers should check the product instructions for suitability with very hot liquids, carbonated drinks, acidic beverages, and microwave use. The cup should not be placed in a microwave or used as a cooking container.

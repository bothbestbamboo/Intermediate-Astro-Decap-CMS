Project Show: Cuiping Mountain Park Tourist Center

  • Design Philosophy:

    Simplicity in Grandeur and Harmony in Emerald Gathering

  • Concept Description:

    The entire building is based on the concept of ancient Oriental philosophy, comprising three circles. The largest circle has a diameter of 111 meters, with the inner one being 87 meters. The largest circle serves as the main functional area for visitor services, while the two smaller circles primarily house viewing walkways and a tourism data center, connecting the city with the scenic area. The architectural style is grounded in the bamboo culture of Yibin, incorporating an abundance of bamboo elements and lines, resulting in a graceful and uniquely elegant design.

  • Scenic Spot Introduction:

    Completed in 2020, the Cuiping Mountain Park Visitor Center has a total construction area of 13,500 square meters and a building height of 24 meters. Cuiping Mountain is renowned for ancient relics such as the Tang Dynasty’s Qianfoyan stone carvings and the Ming Dynasty’s Qianfo Temple and Guanyin Hall. It's considered a sacred Buddhist site in Southern Sichuan and is connected to the Zhenshan Mountain, a famous Daoist cultural mountain in the region. The area is also fabled to be the birthplace of Nezha culture. Scenic Tourist Center by Architectural Design and Research Institute of Southeast University.

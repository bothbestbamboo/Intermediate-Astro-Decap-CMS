Vietnam Bamboo Flooring: Suppliers and Types

Sep 23, 2026, 8:30 AM

With a large selection of bamboo flooring goods that appeal to both domestic and foreign markets, Vietnam has become a major participant in the market. Vietnamese bamboo flooring has grown in popularity among builders and consumers due to its sustainable methods and dedication to quality. This blog explores the several kinds of bamboo flooring that are offered in Vietnam, along with their sizes, top suppliers, and unique selling points.


Bamboo flooring's environmental friendliness is one of its main draws. Bamboo is a great option for customers that care about the environment because it is a renewable resource that can be obtained responsibly. Bamboo flooring comes in a variety of forms in Vietnam, each with special qualities and visual appeal. Strand-woven bamboo flooring, engineered bamboo flooring, and solid bamboo flooring are the most popular varieties.

Whole bamboo stalks are cut and ground into planks to create solid bamboo flooring. This kind of flooring, which highlights the distinctive grain patterns of bamboo, is renowned for its longevity and inherent beauty. Because solid bamboo comes in a variety of finishes, including as natural, carbonised, and stained alternatives, homeowners can select a style that best fits their preferences.


A thin layer of bamboo veneer is adhered to a core of high-density fibreboard (HDF) or plywood to create engineered bamboo flooring. By improving stability, this building technique lessens its vulnerability to changes in temperature and moisture. For spaces like kitchens and bathrooms that experience severe humidity or temperature fluctuations, engineered bamboo is a great option.

Shredded bamboo fibres are compressed under intense pressure to form strand-woven bamboo flooring. A dense, extremely durable flooring solution that can tolerate high foot traffic is produced by this method. Strand-woven bamboo is a flexible option for both residential and commercial settings because it frequently comes in a range of colours and finishes.

Bamboo flooring in Vietnam usually comes in standard boards that are between 120 and 200 mm wide and between 900 and 1800 mm long. Custom sizes, however, can also be made to satisfy certain project specifications. The boards' thickness typically varies between 10 and 20 mm, contingent upon their nature and intended purpose. Because of this variety, builders and homeowners can choose bamboo flooring that best suits their functional and aesthetic requirements.


Bamboo flooring from a number of top Vietnamese providers has earned a reputation for excellence and creativity. Bamboo Mega is a well-known provider, renowned for its wide selection of bamboo flooring products and dedication to sustainability. They provide a range of finishes and colours to fit any décor, in addition to solid and engineered bamboo alternatives.

Vietnam Bamboo Flooring Co., which specialises in strand-woven bamboo flooring, is another well-known provider. Architects and interior designers love their products because of their long-lasting quality and fashionable styles. In order to guarantee that their products have as little of an impact on the environment as possible, they also prioritise environmentally friendly manufacturing techniques.


Furthermore, the Green Bamboo Company has become well-known for its dedication to environmentally friendly operations and premium bamboo flooring. Along with a variety of sizes and finishes, they provide a large assortment of bamboo flooring varieties. They have established a solid reputation in the sector because to their emphasis on employing renewable resources and upholding environmental norms.

The visual adaptability of Vietnam bamboo flooring is one of its best qualities. There is a flooring variety to suit every taste, whether you like the organic appearance of solid bamboo or the modern appeal of strand-woven choices. Bamboo is a popular material for both residential and commercial uses because of its distinctive grain patterns and warm tones, which may enhance the beauty of any room.


Bamboo flooring is also renowned for being resilient and long-lasting. It is an affordable flooring option that may last for many years with proper care. Particularly strand-woven bamboo's hardness makes it impervious to dents, scratches, and other wear, which makes it perfect for high-traffic areas.

When selecting bamboo flooring, installation is still another crucial consideration. Depending on the product and the particular needs of the project, the majority of bamboo flooring types can be installed using the floating method, glue-down, or nail-down ways. Greater adaptability in a variety of contexts, whether it's a new construction or renovation, is made possible by the flexibility of installation techniques.


Reviews on Vietnam bamboo flooring generally point out all of its many benefits, including durability, aesthetic appeal, and sustainability. It's no surprise that bamboo flooring from Vietnam has drawn interest from customers all around the world given the large range of styles and sizes that are available and the top suppliers that are dedicated to quality. Vietnam's bamboo flooring sector is ideally positioned to satisfy the growing demand for fashionable and environmentally friendly flooring solutions and prosper in the international market.

Bamboo Veneer Expands Veneer Industry Species

Sep 12, 2026, 11:30 AM

Bamboo is well known as very green and renewable material all over the world, bamboo has really wide applications, leaf can be extracted for liquid, pole can be treated specially for trees, graves support, stalk can be cut for flooring, plywood manufacturing, we can say every part of bamboo is a precious, no waste, with high technology and computerized equipment, we can control bamboo usage to be over 95%.

In regular veneer industry, it is always wood, like mahogany, teak, maple etc. Wood has the widest species for veneer because wood can be easily sliced or rotatory cut from log to veneer, under current globe environ protection circumstance, wood is limited to cut because it grows so slowly and there is a big waste on wood usage, the bark, the uneven log surface are all cut off. With big improvement of bamboo industry, bothbest works with bamboo and bring bamboo into veneer, not easy but finally make it, bamboo veneer has so many of manufacturing knowledge rather than flooring and plywood.

Bamboo is cut down from the mountain, well treated like boiling the strips to remove the sugar, precise cutting on machine to make the strips be flat enough, gluing with low VOC adhesive to ensure bamboo veneer is healthy, no poison and formaldehyde, laminating by high temperature presser to guarantee it is strong and no breaking, finally slicing the bamboo log into veneer sheets with high-tech slicer machines, very tiny tolerance to make sure each sheet has the same thickness.

Bamboo veneer has so wide applications, furniture covering is the majority because nowadays furniture is not always made of solid wood, too expensive, the economic solution is to cover the veneer on plywood or MDF/HDF, same looking but comfortable pricing. Wood veneer occupies the market in the past, now bamboo veneer comes into the competition and grows up very steadily because it is so beautiful looking, so green material, so flexible dimensions.

Bamboo veneer is so thin and soft, it is very suitable for producing all kinds of fine crafts, for example, paper-cut which is not only a fantastic product, but a great simple of Chinese traditional culture, in the old days, paper-cut was carefully handled on regular paper, with bamboo veneer involved, it gets a total new era, bamboo is so elegant, so graceful, in China, bamboo means upright and noble man of honor. Bamboo paper cut is so dazzling, so brilliant, it is a perfect product with conformity of traditional Chinese culture with modern green material.

There are so many of distinctive products made of bamboo veneer because they all rely on bamboo veneer’s great flexibility, steady quality and environmental pursue. Bamboo can lead the trend of green material, replace the wood to change people’s out-of-date ideas.

## 🏭 Manufacturing Process

Our carbonized bamboo phone stands are manufactured through a controlled, high-precision process[cite: 37]:

1. **Raw Material Selection:** Mature Moso bamboo is harvested at peak age to guarantee optimal density and strength[cite: 37].
2. **Precision Cutting & Carbonization:** Bamboo strips are precision-milled and subjected to high-temperature carbonization for rich color and enhanced moisture resistance[cite: 37].
3. **High-Pressure Lamination:** Selected strips are bonded using eco-friendly adhesives and pressed under heavy hydraulic force into solid block material[cite: 37].
4. **CNC Milling & Slotting:** Automated CNC equipment carves out the precise device slot and cable routing channels[cite: 37].
5. **Surface Polishing & Sealing:** Hand-sanded to remove all burrs and coated with a protective clear varnish for a smooth, moisture-resistant finish[cite: 37].

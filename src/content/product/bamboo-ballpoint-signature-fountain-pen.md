## 📝 Product Overview

The **Bamboo Ballpoint, Signature, and Fountain Pen Set** brings three writing instruments into a single, cohesive collection. Each barrel is turned from solid Moso bamboo and finished in a warm carbonized tone, then paired with polished gold-plated fittings — clip, mid-ring, and tip cone — for a quietly confident look on any desk.

Three nib styles cover the way most people actually write. The ballpoint is the workhorse for everyday notes. The signature pen (gel/rollerball) lays down a smoother, more expressive line for journal entries and personal letters. The fountain pen, fitted with a stainless steel nib in a gold finish, suits longer sessions, calligraphy practice, and the kind of writing where ink colour and line variation matter. The three pens share an identical silhouette, which makes the set read as a unified gift rather than three mismatched items.

A hinged bamboo gift case is included. The case measures 17.3 × 5.6 × 2.3 cm, with a 15 cm long × 1.3 cm wide contoured slot that holds any of the three pens securely, closed by a small concealed magnet.

## 🏭 Manufacturing Process

Each pen is produced through a controlled, repeatable workshop process:

1. **Culm Selection:** Mature Moso bamboo poles (typically 4–6 years old) are selected for wall thickness, straightness, and tight node spacing.
2. **Strip Cutting and Drying:** Poles are split into strips and kiln-dried to a stable moisture content to prevent later cracking or shrinkage.
3. **Carbonization:** Strips are steamed and heat-treated to bring the colour from pale yellow to the warm caramel tone that defines the finished pen.
4. **Barrel Turning:** Each barrel is CNC-turned to final diameter and length, then sanded in graded passes to a smooth, splinter-free surface.
5. **Fitting Assembly:** Gold-plated clips, mid-rings, and tip cones are pressed into place; the internal mechanism — twist, gel refill, or fountain converter — is fitted and tested.
6. **Lacquer and Polish:** A thin, low-VOC lacquer is applied for moisture resistance, followed by a hand polish to a soft sheen.
7. **Nib Tuning (Fountain Pen):** The steel nib is aligned, flow-tested, and inspected for consistent line width before assembly.
8. **Case Packing:** Each pen is paired with its bamboo case, checked against a writing test, and packed for shipment.

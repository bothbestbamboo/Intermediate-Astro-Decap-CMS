SPC (Stone Plastic Composite) flooring is a cutting-edge, high-performance flooring option designed for both residential and commercial spaces. Its core is composed of a unique blend of limestone, polyvinyl chloride (PVC), and stabilizers, giving it exceptional strength and durability. This multi-layered construction consists of a wear layer, a vinyl top layer, the rigid SPC core, and often a pre-attached underlayment, providing a range of benefits that make it a popular choice.

One of the standout features of SPC flooring is its waterproof nature. Unlike traditional hardwood, laminate, or even some vinyl flooring options, SPC is completely impervious to water, making it ideal for moisture-prone areas like kitchens, bathrooms, laundry rooms, and basements. Its durability also shines in high-traffic areas, as the SPC flooring is resistant to scratches, dents, and stains, maintaining its appearance even under heavy use.

The rigid SPC core offers superior dimensional stability, ensuring the flooring doesn’t warp, expand, or contract due to changes in temperature or humidity. This makes it a great option for areas with fluctuating climates or where temperature control is a challenge. Installation is user-friendly, often featuring a click-lock system that requires no adhesives or nails. It can be laid over most existing subfloors, further simplifying the process.

**Carbonized Strand Woven Bamboo Tabletops** are engineered for exceptional strength, eco-chic beauty, and maximum durability. ## 💎 Key Advantages

* **Unrivaled Structural Hardness:** Rated at 70.4 GPa hardness and 1.23 g/cm³ density, strand woven bamboo outperforms conventional woods like Teak, Merbau, and Cedar against scratches and everyday wear.
* **Superior Moisture & Weather Resistance:** With an exceptionally low 24-hour swelling rate of just 0.35% and 4.7% low moisture content, it effectively resists warping, cracking, and outdoor humidity changes.
* **Rich Carbonized Aesthetics:** Thermal carbonization caramelizes the natural bamboo sugars, producing a rich, warm brown tone that penetrates throughout the panel rather than just a surface stain.
* **Precision Smooth Edging:** Features professionally polished, rounded edges that deliver comfortable wrist support and a safe, sleek profile.

---

## 🏭 Manufacturing Process

Our strand woven bamboo tabletops undergo a rigorous thermal-mechanical transformation to achieve engineering-grade density and stability:

1. **Fiber Extraction & Strip Preparation:** Mature Moso bamboo stalks are harvested, shredded into loose bamboo strands, and dried.
2. **Thermal Carbonization:** Bamboo fibers undergo high-pressure steam treatment to achieve the dark caramelized shade and eliminate natural starches to prevent decay.
3. **Extreme High-Pressure Compression:** Dried strands are mixed with eco-friendly resin and hot-pressed under thousands of tons of pressure into solid, ultra-dense composite blocks.
4. **Precision CNC Milling & Edging:** Solid blocks are sliced into exact tabletop dimensions (20mm thickness) and milled with smooth rounded corners.
5. **Surface Coating & Quality Inspection:** Panels receive multiple protective seal coats for UV and moisture resistance before final quality inspection and protective packaging.

## 📝 Product Overview

Our **Multi-Layer Rolled Bamboo Sleeves for Office Tumblers** redefine sustainable drinkware design by replacing traditional solid bamboo shells with advanced veneer-wrapping engineering. Crafted specifically for insulated office tumblers and travel mugs, these sleeves deliver a smooth, natural bamboo tactile experience while eliminating the high cracking and warping risks associated with traditional solid bamboo.

Constructed using high-precision multi-layer rolling technology paired with **German Jowat food-grade hot-melt adhesive**, each sleeve achieves exceptional dimensional accuracy, high structural strength, and outstanding moisture resistance reaching the **European D4 waterproof standard**. It provides a premium, eco-chic natural finish that seamlessly integrates with stainless steel and colorful powder-coated tumbler bodies.

## 💎 Key Advantages

* **Crack-Proof Multi-Layer Rolling Technology:** Unlike natural solid bamboo shells that crack easily when exposed to liquid or temperature changes, our rolled veneer structure distributes tension evenly to eliminate cracking risks entirely.
* **Premium German Jowat Food-Grade Adhesive:** Bonded with genuine Jowat hot-melt adhesive, guaranteeing high bond strength, complete safety for food-contact applications, and full compliance with international safety standards.
* **European D4 Waterproof Performance:** Meets stringent European DIN EN 204 D4 waterproof requirements, ensuring the sleeve will not peel, de-laminate, or degrade during daily washing or moist environments.

## 🏭 Manufacturing Process

Our bamboo tumbler sleeves undergo a specialized technical process to balance natural beauty with industrial longevity:

1. **Veneer Selection & Slicing:** Premium natural bamboo is sliced into ultra-thin, flexible wood veneers with consistent grain pattern and color.
2. **Adhesive Application:** Food-grade German Jowat hot-melt adhesive is evenly applied across bamboo layers under strict temperature control.
3. **Multi-Layer Roll-Wrapping:** Veneers are tightly wrapped in continuous cross-laminated layers onto precision cylindrical molds.
4. **Thermal Bonding & Pressing:** High pressure and heat cure the adhesive, creating a seamless, ultra-dense cylindrical shell.
5. **Precision Trimming & CNC Polishing:** Shells are cut to exact φ82x115mm measurements, smooth-sanded, and treated with protective food-safe surface sealing.

Bamboo Flooring Philippines Supplier

Sep 12, 2026, 11:30 AM

Bamboo flooring has gained immense popularity in recent years, especially in the Philippines, where sustainability and eco-friendliness are highly valued. This natural material not only offers aesthetic appeal but also presents a durable and versatile flooring option. In this blog, we will explore different types of bamboo flooring, their sizes, leading suppliers in the Philippines, and reviews from users who have embraced this flooring solution.

When it comes to bamboo flooring, there are primarily two types: solid bamboo and engineered bamboo. Solid bamboo flooring is made from whole bamboo stalks that are cut and processed into planks. This type offers a more authentic look and feel, making it a favorite among homeowners. On the other hand, engineered bamboo flooring consists of a thin layer of bamboo veneer over a plywood base, providing greater stability and resistance to moisture. This makes it an ideal choice for areas with fluctuating humidity levels, such as kitchens and bathrooms.


Sizes of bamboo flooring planks can vary significantly, allowing for flexibility in design. Typically, bamboo planks come in widths ranging from 3 inches to 7 inches, with lengths varying from 36 inches to 78 inches. Wider planks can create a more spacious appearance, while narrower planks offer a classic and traditional look. It’s essential to consider the overall aesthetic of your space when choosing the size of your bamboo flooring, as it can greatly influence the final outcome of your interior design.


In the Philippines, several leading suppliers specialize in bamboo flooring, providing a range of products to fit various budgets and preferences. One of the most recognized suppliers is **Bamboo Home**, known for its high-quality bamboo flooring options that appeal to both modern and traditional styles. They offer a variety of finishes and colors, allowing customers to find the perfect match for their homes.


Another notable supplier is **Philippine Zhu Bamboo**, which prides itself on promoting local bamboo craftsmanship. Their flooring products emphasize sustainability, with a focus on sourcing bamboo from local farmers. This not only supports the local economy but also ensures that the materials used are environmentally friendly. Customers appreciate the unique designs and finishes offered by Philippine Bamboo, making it a popular choice among eco-conscious homeowners.


**Modern Bamboo Flooring Philippines** is also a key player in the market, providing a wide selection of bamboo flooring options at competitive prices. They emphasize customer satisfaction and offer a range of services, including installation and maintenance advice. Reviews from customers highlight the durability and beauty of their products, often noting how well the flooring holds up in various environments.


When considering bamboo flooring, it’s crucial to evaluate not only the products but also the experiences of those who have used them. Reviews from consumers often highlight the aesthetic appeal of bamboo flooring, noting its natural beauty and unique patterns. Many users express satisfaction with the variety of colors and finishes available, allowing them to customize their spaces to their liking.

Durability is another factor frequently mentioned in reviews. Bamboo flooring is known for its hardness, making it resistant to wear and tear. Many homeowners report that their bamboo floors have held up well under heavy foot traffic, maintaining their appearance over time with proper care. However, some users have pointed out that while bamboo is generally durable, it is essential to choose high-quality products from reputable suppliers to avoid issues like scratching or denting.


Moisture resistance is a common concern among homeowners, especially in humid climates like the Philippines. Engineered bamboo flooring is often recommended for areas prone to moisture, as it tends to perform better than solid bamboo in such conditions. Reviews indicate that many users have successfully installed engineered bamboo flooring in kitchens and bathrooms without experiencing significant issues related to moisture damage.


Installation is another aspect that consumers frequently discuss in their reviews. Many suppliers offer professional installation services, which can significantly reduce the stress associated with laying down new flooring. Additionally, some users have opted for DIY installations, sharing their experiences and tips online. Overall, reviews suggest that with the right tools and preparation, installing bamboo flooring can be a manageable project for those willing to take it on.

In terms of maintenance, bamboo flooring is relatively easy to care for. Most consumers recommend regular sweeping or vacuuming to remove dirt and debris, along with occasional mopping with a damp cloth. Some users have noted that using specific cleaning products formulated for bamboo can help maintain the finish and extend the life of the flooring. Overall, reviews indicate that with minimal effort, bamboo floors can remain beautiful for many years.


In conclusion, bamboo flooring presents an attractive and sustainable option for homeowners in the Philippines. With various types, sizes, and suppliers available, it’s essential to explore the options and consider personal preferences when selecting the perfect flooring for your space. Positive reviews from consumers highlight not only the beauty and durability of bamboo flooring but also the importance of choosing reliable suppliers for the best results. Whether you are renovating your home or building a new one, bamboo flooring could be the ideal solution to enhance your interior design.

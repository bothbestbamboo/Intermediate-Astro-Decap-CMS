Bamboo Door Made of Bamboo Plywood

Sep 12, 2026, 11:30 AM

Bamboo door is produced from solid bamboo panel. Our bamboo panel is UF-free and zero bending. Bamboo door is manufactured with kinds of styles and designs suitable for interior and exterior doors. Bamboo doors are constructed using strips of bamboo that are laminated together under high pressure, creating a sturdy and resilient surface. The natural grain and color variations of bamboo give each door a unique and organic appearance.

Bamboo door is much more environmental and eco-friendly than regular wooden door or MDF door because bamboo is a plantation resources and bamboo can help the world and human being reduce carbon emission. Bamboo doors can be customized to suit various design preferences. They can be stained or painted to match existing decor, or left natural for a more rustic or modern look.

Bamboo door color: Natural or Carbonized

Bamboo doors size:

  • 1900 x 670 x 40 mm
  • 2000 x 800 x 40 mm

Features of bamboo door:

  • Made from 100% bamboo
  • Designed to match any bamboo flooring for your home
  • Available for pre-hung
  • Fire proof, heat isolation, moth proof, corrosion resistance

​Bamboo Flooring in Colombia

Sep 23, 2026, 8:30 AM

​

Bamboo flooring has gained popularity in Colombia as an eco-friendly and stylish alternative to traditional hardwood flooring. Its unique combination of durability, aesthetic appeal, and sustainability makes it an attractive option for homeowners across the country. In this blog, we will explore the most popular bamboo flooring types and colors in major Colombian cities, address common concerns among end users, and recommend installation methods suitable for the local environment.

Bamboo flooring presents an exciting, sustainable option for homeowners in Colombia, offering a blend of beauty, durability, and affordability. With reputable suppliers, a variety of types and colors, and flexible installation methods, it’s easy to find the perfect bamboo flooring to suit any style and budget.

As consumers become more eco-conscious, bamboo flooring is likely to continue gaining popularity, making it a smart investment for both current and future homeowners. By understanding the available options, warranties, and purchasing methods, buyers can confidently choose bamboo flooring that enhances their living spaces while supporting sustainable practices.

Popular Bamboo Flooring Types in Colombia

1. Strand-Woven Bamboo

Strand-woven bamboo is known for its remarkable durability. This type is created by compressing bamboo fibers under high pressure, resulting in a flooring option that is often harder than traditional hardwoods. With a Janka hardness rating typically ranging between 2,000 and 3,000, strand-woven bamboo is ideal for high-traffic areas. Its strength makes it suitable for homes with children and pets, making it a popular choice in urban centers like Bogotá and Medellín.

2. Horizontal Bamboo

Horizontal bamboo flooring showcases the natural beauty of bamboo stalks, cut horizontally to display their unique grain patterns. This type has a classic look and is favored for its warmth and inviting appearance. It works well in traditional and contemporary settings, making it a versatile choice for homes in cities such as Cali and Barranquilla.

3. Vertical Bamboo

Vertical bamboo flooring is cut vertically, resulting in a more consistent grain pattern. This type provides a sleek, modern look and is favored for smaller spaces, as it can create an illusion of height. Homeowners in urban areas like Cartagena often choose vertical bamboo to enhance the visual appeal of compact living spaces.

​Popular Colors of Bamboo Flooring

The color options for bamboo flooring are diverse, allowing homeowners to choose styles that best fit their interior design preferences. Here are some of the most sought-after colors in Brazil:

Natural Bamboo

Natural bamboo flooring retains its original light tan color, highlighting the material's natural beauty. This option is versatile and can complement various decor styles, making it a popular choice in cities like Bogotá, where diverse design aesthetics are embraced.

Caramelized Bamboo

Caramelized bamboo is achieved through a heat treatment process that enhances the color, resulting in a warm, rich hue. This option adds depth to interiors and is especially favored in regions like Medellín, where homeowners often seek a cozy and inviting ambiance.

Carbonized Bamboo

Carbonized bamboo undergoes a more intense heat treatment, resulting in a dark, rich color. This option provides a striking contrast to lighter furnishings and can add a dramatic flair to any room. Carbonized bamboo is commonly chosen by homeowners in Barranquilla and Cali, where bold design choices are celebrated.

Stained Bamboo

Stained bamboo flooring allows for further customization, as it can be treated with various stains to achieve desired colors. Popular choices include gray, espresso, and whitewashed finishes. Stained bamboo is particularly popular in Cartagena, where contemporary design trends flourish.

​Common Concerns Among End Users

1. Durability and Scratch Resistance

One of the primary concerns for homeowners is the durability of bamboo flooring. While strand-woven bamboo is exceptionally durable, some types may be more susceptible to scratches and dents. Customers should consider their lifestyle, especially if they have pets or children, when selecting the appropriate bamboo type.

2. Moisture Resistance

Humidity is a significant factor in many regions of Colombia, particularly in coastal areas. Homeowners often worry about bamboo’s susceptibility to moisture. Engineered bamboo is recommended for humid environments, as it is designed to resist warping and damage. This makes it a preferred choice for homes in Buenaventura and Cartagena.

3. Maintenance Requirements

Potential buyers frequently inquire about the maintenance of bamboo flooring. Bamboo is relatively low-maintenance compared to hardwood, but it does require care to maintain its appearance. Regular sweeping and occasional damp mopping with a gentle cleaner are recommended. Homeowners should avoid harsh chemicals that can damage the finish.

4. Installation Complexity

Many end users express concerns about the installation process. While bamboo flooring can be installed as a DIY project, hiring a professional is often recommended for optimal results. This is especially true for engineered bamboo, which may require specific installation techniques to ensure stability.

Recommended Installation Methods for Colombia

Glue-Down Installation

This method involves applying adhesive directly to the subfloor and laying the bamboo planks on top. Glue-down installation is particularly effective for engineered bamboo, as it helps to secure the flooring in place and minimizes movement. This method is suitable for both concrete and wooden subfloors, making it versatile for various construction types commonly found in Colombian homes.

Nail-Down Installation

Nail-down installation is commonly used for solid bamboo flooring. This method involves nailing the planks directly to a wooden subfloor. While it can be labor-intensive, it provides a secure fit and is ideal for high-traffic areas, making it a practical option for busy households.

Floating Installation

The floating method is becoming increasingly popular, especially for engineered bamboo. In this technique, the planks are not attached to the subfloor but rather interlock with each other. This allows for natural expansion and contraction, making it suitable for various climates. Floating installations are particularly favored in coastal regions like Buenaventura, where humidity can cause traditional installations to warp.

Regional Preferences

Different cities in Colombia exhibit unique preferences for bamboo flooring based on climate, lifestyle, and design trends:

  • Bogotá: Urban dwellers in Bogotá often opt for strand-woven or natural bamboo due to their durability and modern aesthetic. The fast-paced lifestyle demands flooring that can withstand wear while maintaining visual appeal.
  • Medellín: Homeowners here frequently choose caramelized bamboo, aligning with the city’s vibrant culture. The warm tones complement the artistic ambiance prevalent in Medellín's interior designs.
  • Cali: Known for its festive atmosphere, Cali showcases a preference for carbonized bamboo, which adds a bold element to homes. The dramatic color choices reflect the city’s lively character.
  • Barranquilla: In Barranquilla, where coastal living is prominent, residents often choose engineered bamboo for its moisture resistance. This practical choice helps combat the effects of humidity in the region.
  • Cartagena: As a tourist hotspot, Cartagena features a blend of design influences, with stained bamboo options being particularly popular. Homeowners often experiment with various finishes to create unique and stylish interiors.
  • Buenaventura: In this coastal city, engineered bamboo is favored for its stability in humid conditions. Homeowners prioritize durability and moisture resistance, making it a practical choice for their homes.

Janka Hardness Rating: The Janka hardness rating measures the resistance of a wood or bamboo species to wear and denting. Bamboo flooring typically has a Janka hardness rating ranging from 1,300 to 1,600 pounds-force (lbf), depending on the type of Bamboo flooring and the manufacturing process. This rating can help determine the durability of the flooring.

Moisture Content: Bamboo flooring should have a moisture content of around 6% to 9% before installation to prevent warping, buckling, or other issues. Proper acclimatization of the flooring to the installation environment is crucial to ensure a stable and long-lasting installation.

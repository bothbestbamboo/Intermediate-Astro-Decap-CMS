SPC Floor: Innovative Materials Leading the New Trend of Flooring

Sep 12, 2026, 11:30 AM

In today's building materials market, a new material - SPC stone crystal floor is rapidly rising with its unique advantages and has become the focus of attention of many consumers and the decoration industry. So, what material is SPC stone crystal floor? What are its characteristics and advantages? Let us walk into the world of SPC stone crystal floor and explore the mystery of this innovative material.


I. Definition and composition of SPC stone crystal floor

SPC stone crystal floor, the full name is Stone Plastic Composite, that is, stone plastic composite material. It is mainly made of natural stone powder and polyvinyl chloride (PVC) resin as the main raw materials, and is made through high-temperature melting, extrusion molding and other processes.

Natural stone powder gives SPC stone crystal floor hard and wear-resistant properties, while polyvinyl chloride resin gives it good flexibility and waterproof properties. In addition, some special additives, such as stabilizers and lubricants, are added to SPC stone crystal floor to improve its performance and processing technology.

II. Characteristics of SPC crystal floor

  • Super stability

    SPC crystal floor has extremely high stability and will not expand, shrink or deform due to changes in temperature and humidity. This makes it very suitable for use in a variety of different environments, whether in the hot south or the cold north, it can maintain a good condition.

  • Excellent waterproof performance

    SPC crystal floor has excellent waterproof performance. Because its main components are stone powder and polyvinyl chloride resin, it does not contain any wood components, so it is not as easy to get wet and deform as traditional wooden floors. Even in a humid environment, SPC crystal floor can remain dry, providing users with a comfortable and safe living environment.

  • High wear resistance

    The surface of SPC crystal floor has been specially treated and has very high wear resistance. It can resist daily wear and scratches, and can maintain long-term beauty even in places with high traffic. This feature makes SPC crystal floor very suitable for commercial places and home decoration.

  • Environmental protection and health

    SPC crystal floor is made of environmentally friendly materials, does not contain harmful substances such as formaldehyde, and meets national environmental protection standards. During the production process, no substances harmful to the environment will be produced. It is a truly green and environmentally friendly material.

  • Easy installation

    SPC crystal floor adopts a lock-type installation method, without the use of glue or other adhesives, and the installation process is very simple and quick. Moreover, this installation method can also avoid the environmental pollution caused by the use of glue.

  • Rich patterns and styles

    SPC crystal floor has a rich variety of patterns and styles to meet the personalized needs of different consumers. Whether it is a simple modern style, a European classical style or a traditional Chinese style, you can find a matching SPC crystal floor.


III. Comparison between SPC crystal floor and similar materials

  • Compared with traditional wooden floors

    Traditional wooden floors are easily affected by temperature and humidity, and are prone to expansion, contraction and deformation. SPC crystal floor has super stability and will not have these problems. In addition, traditional wooden floors require regular waxing and maintenance, while SPC crystal flooring does not require special maintenance and can be cleaned daily. In terms of environmental protection, traditional wooden floors may contain harmful substances such as formaldehyde, while SPC crystal flooring is environmentally friendly and formaldehyde-free.

  • Compared with ceramic tiles

    Although ceramic tiles are hard and wear-resistant, they are easy to slip and feel hard on the feet. SPC crystal flooring has anti-slip properties and feels comfortable on the feet. In terms of installation, ceramic tiles require professional workers to lay them, while SPC crystal flooring can be installed by yourself, which is very convenient. In addition, SPC crystal flooring has more patterns and styles, which can meet the needs of different decoration styles.

IV. Application areas of SPC crystal flooring

  • Home decoration

    SPC crystal flooring can be used in various rooms of the home, such as living room, bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, etc. It not only has a beautiful appearance, but also provides a comfortable and safe living environment for the family.

  • Commercial places

    SPC crystal flooring is very suitable for commercial places, such as shopping malls, hotels, office buildings, restaurants, etc. Its high wear resistance and waterproof performance can meet the needs of high traffic and frequent cleaning in commercial places.

  • Public places

    SPC stone crystal floor can also be used in public places such as schools, hospitals, libraries, gymnasiums, etc. Its environmental health performance and stability can provide people with a safe and comfortable public environment.


V. Consumer evaluation of SPC stone crystal floor

In order to understand the real evaluation of consumers on SPC stone crystal floor, we interviewed several consumers who have used SPC stone crystal floor.

  • Consumer Mr. Li said: "I chose SPC Flooring when I renovated my home, and the effect is very good. The floor is very stable and there is no deformation or cracking after installation. The pattern is also very beautiful, which matches the decoration style of my home very well. Most importantly, it is very environmentally friendly and has no odor, which makes me and my family feel at ease."
  • Consumer Ms. Zhang said: "I chose SPC stone crystal flooring when I renovated my office, mainly considering its waterproof performance and wear resistance. The office has a large flow of people, and liquids such as tea are often spilled on the ground. SPC stone crystal flooring can fully cope with these situations. It is also very convenient to install, saving a lot of time and cost."
  • Consumer Uncle Wang praised the environmental and healthy performance of SPC stone crystal flooring: "I am old and have higher environmental requirements for decoration materials. SPC stone crystal flooring has no odor and does not contain harmful substances such as formaldehyde, which makes me and my family feel at ease. And the quality of the floor is also very good and durable."

From the evaluation of consumers, it can be seen that SPC stone crystal flooring has won the recognition and praise of consumers for its excellent performance and environmental and healthy characteristics.

Summary: SPC flooring is an innovative material made of natural stone powder and polyvinyl chloride resin. It is stable, waterproof, wear-resistant, environmentally friendly and easy to install. Compared with similar materials, it has obvious advantages, wide applications and broad market prospects.

Bamboo Flooring in Los Angeles

Sep 23, 2026, 8:30 AM

US is the biggest market for bamboo flooring and containers of bamboo flooring can be sold every day. Homedepot, Walnut, Target, Lowes all carry bamboo flooring in their chain-stores, plus hundreds and thousands of flooring stores are selling bamboo as well. Bothbest has seen a very huge quantity increasing on our premium bamboo flooring, the shipment from China to US takes about 14 days, plus 3 days for Customs, 3-7 days for transportation, it takes quite long time to arrive at the customers. The shipment from factory to private is pretty complicated because of Customs involved. Private person can import overseas goods directly, but several papers are required. Many private are not worrying about the papers, but the goods, because money is transferred to overseas factory, most of people are thinking if the factory will make their promise to make the delivery. This is the biggest obstacle between private with factory.

Now, Bothbest carries large stock of various types of bamboo flooring in the warehouse in Los Angeles and sell bamboo flooring nationwide with Los Angeles as the headquarter. Currently, we are carrying out most popular bamboo - strand woven bamboo flooring, both standard tongue and groove and click profile. Tongue and groove is standard and can be installed everywhere. Click profile is glueless installation and increasing fast than T+G.

Bothbest has very strong developing on strand woven bamboo and we expanded the types of bamboo, from natural, caramel color to various stains - chocolate, walnut, charcoal. Plus, we use strand woven bamboo as top layer, laminate with HDF and make very new and good price bamboo flooring. We will bring more containers of other bamboo in.

In our warehouse, we can accept any quantity of order and below price is FOB Los Angeles warehouse. You can arrange the pick-up bamboo at our warehouse or we can arrange the shipping for you.

Disposable & Reusable Bamboo Chopsticks

Sep 12, 2026, 11:30 AM

Chopsticks have a long history in my country and have been a necessity for every family since ancient times. There are many varieties of chopsticks on the market, including solid wood chopsticks, traditional bamboo chopsticks, stainless steel and steel-plastic chopsticks, etc. There are countless. Different types of chopsticks have different production processes.




Stainless steel and steel-plastic chopsticks are too heavy, too smooth, and have a poor feel. Bamboo chopsticks and wooden chopsticks have a good feel and moderate weight. Today, the world is advocating the concept of green environmental protection, and steel-plastic chopsticks obviously violate this concept.



Traditional bamboo chopsticks and wooden chopsticks are prone to mold and deterioration, which troubles the people. Traditional wooden chopsticks and bamboo chopsticks are more or less made of chemical raw materials to treat mildew, corrosion, and insects. Bamboo and wooden chopsticks treated with chemical raw materials have more or less chemical residues, and the thermo treated bamboo chopsticks invented by our company that have been heat-treated at high temperature have solved the above problems very well. Shuangwanfu bamboo chopsticks solves the defects of traditional bamboo and wooden chopsticks and avoids the risks brought by the use of chemical raw materials. The high-temperature modified bamboo charcoal chopsticks have the following characteristics:

  • No need to add any chemical treatment.
  • No need to paint or color.
  • The chopsticks are mildew-proof and antibacterial.
  • They can quickly and automatically release moisture, and the expansion coefficient is extremely low after being boiled in boiling water.
  • They feel great when used, and are a kind of green and environmentally friendly functional chopsticks.

According to research: Bamboo charcoal powder is rich in calcium, potassium, minerals and various trace elements required by the human body, which can enhance the body's immunity and prevent diseases. It also has the effects of detoxification and stomach cleansing, and can help absorb various harmful substances in the human body and then excrete them out of the body. At the same time, it can also improve skin dryness, itching and other conditions, and is also very effective for skin allergies, infections and other symptoms. In addition, it has a good adsorption function and can be used for water purification, deodorization and air purification. Shuangwanfu bamboo chopsticks look like an ordinary pair of chopsticks, but they have advantages that other chopsticks do not have.



After using and washing the Shuangwanfu bamboo chopsticks, there is no need to disinfect them in a disinfection cabinet, etc. They only need to be disinfected in 100-degree boiling water, which is very convenient.

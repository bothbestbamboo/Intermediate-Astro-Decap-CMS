## 🏭 Manufacturing Process

Our natural bamboo straws are crafted through a strict chemical-free process:

1. **Selective Harvesting:** Fully matured Moso bamboo poles are harvested from sustainably managed forests.
2. **Precision Slicing & Sizing:** Bamboo poles are cut into precise 200mm lengths and sorted into distinct diameter categories (6mm, 7mm, 8mm, 9mm, 11mm).
3. **High-Temperature Boiling & Sterilization:** Raw straws are boiled using pure traditional thermal steam processes to sterilize, extract natural sugars, and prevent mold growth without relying on harsh chemicals.
4. **Precision Polishing & Beveling:** End profiles are mechanically turned and hand-inspected to create ultra-smooth, burr-free chamfered edges.
5. **Drying & Laser Engraving:** Straws undergo natural kiln-drying before undergoing optional precision laser logo engraving according to customer specifications.
6. **Final Hygiene Inspection & Paper Box Packaging:** Inspected pieces are clean-packed into protective, eco-friendly kraft paper gift boxes.

Bamboo decking or terrace bamboo flooring is installed outside, like garden, swimming pool, public area because it is anti-moldy, long-life flooring. Bamboo decking offers a unique blend of beauty, durability, and sustainability, making it an excellent choice for outdoor spaces. Made from compressed bamboo fibers, bamboo decking is highly resistant to weather, insects, and decay, making it ideal for use in decks, patios, and other outdoor areas.

Bamboo decking is made out of strand woven bamboo flooring and we do the oil on the decking, so it will be water proof and it can last a long time. Bamboo fibers are very strong, making bamboo decking resistant to scratches, dents, and fading from sunlight. This durability ensures that your bamboo deck will look beautiful for years to come with minimal maintenance. A range of colors, including light carbon, standard carbon, dark carbon and brown, allowing you to customize the look of your outdoor space. Its natural grain patterns add a touch of organic beauty to any deck, creating a warm and inviting outdoor living area.

Strand woven bamboo has very unique design and the density is much higher than regular bamboo, for outdoor purpose, the density - 1,100kg/m3 is the highest in all of bamboo series, so it is really strong and it is not so easy to be bending or cupping.

Bamboo decking dimension:

  • 1860 x 140 x 18 mm

Bamboo Decking Color: Carbonized only

Hardness: 3000 per Janka scale

Glue: Dynea, European 1 standard

Finish: OSMO oil, Germany brand

Milling: Pre-oiled with grooves

Installation: Nail, or Sub-floor

All bamboo flooring is characterized by:

  • Lowest formaldehyde in the industry ( 0.0127 ppm for standard bamboo flooring, 0.02 ppm for strand woven bamboo flooring )
  • Use of only sustainably harvested 5-8 year old bamboo
  • Milled with the finest German and Italian equipment for greatest consistency
  • All bamboo floors available unfinished or prefinished
  • 25-year non-delamination warranty
  • 5-year "wear through" warranty on Aluminum Oxide finished bamboo flooring

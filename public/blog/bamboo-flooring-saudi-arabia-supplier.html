Bamboo Flooring Saudi Arabia Supplier

Sep 12, 2026, 11:30 AM

The growing desire for eco-friendly and fashionable interior design has led to a steady increase in the popularity of bamboo flooring in Saudi Arabia. Bamboo is leading the way in the trend away from traditional hardwoods and toward more environmentally friendly options, which you have probably noticed as a factory technologist. The present state of bamboo flooring in Saudi Arabia is examined in this blog, along with product categories, significant distributors and suppliers, warranty procedures, and pricing ranges.


Comprehending Bamboo Saudi Arabian flooring market

Bamboo is a fast-growing grass that resembles hardwood in both appearance and functionality, but it is not a wood. It is renowned for being aesthetically pleasing, long-lasting, and sustainable. Flooring materials must endure high temperatures and low humidity in arid, hot climates like Saudi Arabia. When properly acclimated and installed, bamboo—especially strand-woven varieties—performs fairly well in these conditions.


Common Types of Bamboo Flooring in Saudi Arabia

There are numerous varieties of bamboo flooring available in the Saudi market, each meeting distinct functional and aesthetic needs:


1. Bamboo that is horizontal

This kind offers a classic and natural appearance by displaying the bamboo stalk's natural knuckles. It is more common in residential settings and is usually offered in carbonized or natural finishes.

2. Bamboo Vertical

Vertical bamboo looks more homogeneous and linear, making it a good fit for contemporary spaces. Its structure makes it slightly harder than horizontal bamboo, though both are suitable for light to moderate foot traffic.

3. Strand-Woven Bamboo

Made by compressing shredded bamboo fibers under intense pressure, strand-woven bamboo is the hardest and most durable type. It is ideal for commercial spaces or homes with heavy foot traffic. This type is increasingly favored in Saudi Arabia due to its resilience against temperature fluctuations.

4. Engineered Bamboo

This flooring type features a bamboo veneer over a high-density fiberboard (HDF) core. It is more dimensionally stable, making it a suitable choice for floating installations or areas with underfloor heating.


Major Suppliers and Distributors in Saudi Arabia

Several local and international players supply bamboo flooring across the Kingdom. A handful of the more noteworthy names include:


1. Al Sorayai Trading and Industrial Group

Headquartered in Jeddah, Al Sorayai has a substantial footprint in the flooring sector. They sell bamboo flooring through their premium product lines and serve both retail and wholesale clientele.

2. Al-Jazera Factory for Home Supplies

Al-Jazera, a Riyadh-based company, mostly imports bamboo flooring from China and Southeast Asia. They specialize in interior design projects for homes and offices and are renowned for their affordable prices.

3. The Floorworld Saudi Arabia

With locations in Riyadh and Dammam, Floorworld is a well-known regional flooring firm. They have a large assortment of strand-woven and engineered bamboo flooring, frequently from reputable companies like EcoFusion and Teragren.

4. Carpets by Al Nafie

Al Nafie has expanded into hard surface flooring, such as bamboo, despite having historically concentrated on carpets. Their items, which have guarantees in line with international standards, are primarily imported from China and Malaysia.


Standards for Quality and Warranty

Bamboo flooring quality assurance differs based on the product's origin and supplier. The supply chain is dominated by Chinese producers, some of whom provide high-end alternatives while others could skimp on milling accuracy or adhesive quality.


In Saudi Arabia, reliable vendors usually offer:

  • Residential warranties range from five to twenty-five years, depending on the bamboo kind and manufacturer.
  • Guarantees of moisture resistance, particularly for engineered and strand-woven bamboo.
  • Finish guarantees, which cover top coat wear-through for up to ten years for finishes made of aluminum oxide or UV curing.

Customers are growing increasingly conscious of the significance of certifications like ISO 9001, E1 (low formaldehyde emissions), and FSC (Forest Stewardship Council).

Cost Range for Bamboo Flooring

Depending on the type, manufacturer, and installation technique, prices in Saudi Arabia can vary significantly. An outline of the general pricing ranges is shown below:


  • Bamboo, both vertical and horizontal: SAR 60–100 per square meter
  • Bamboo strand-woven: 90–180 SAR per square meter
  • Bamboo that has been engineered: SAR 120–200 per square meter

Usually not included in these costs is installation, which can cost anywhere from SAR 20 to SAR 50 per square meter, depending on the size and complexity of the project. Edge trims, moisture barriers, and underlayment could be extra expenses.

Consumer Preferences and Market Trends

Eco-friendly materials are becoming more and more in demand, especially in big cities like Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dammam. Bamboo is being used in high-end residential and hospitality buildings by interior designers and real estate developers. A predilection for minimalist and airy aesthetics is reflected in the current trend toward lighter tints like natural and bleached bamboo.

Consumers' major concerns are environmental certifications and moisture stability. Consequently, strand-woven and engineered bamboo products are becoming more popular, particularly when combined with adhesives free of formaldehyde.

Concluding Remarks for Industry Experts

In Saudi Arabia, bamboo flooring is becoming more popular than it was a few years ago. To guarantee long-term performance, technicians must comprehend the subtleties of each flooring kind, including sourcing, quality control, and acclimation. Bamboo's standing as a practical and fashionable flooring option in the area will be strengthened by working with reputable distributors and instructing customers on how to maintain their products.
